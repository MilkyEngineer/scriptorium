**Status:** passed (steps 1–6) on 2026-10-17; step 7 (rmink-produced ink) deferred to M3. **Client:** fork tag `scriptorium-s0`. **Environment:** a scratch folder inside a real library on the free tier, with the kill switch from `docs/Plan/README.md` (root snapshot before every write, abort if anything other than the run's own UUIDs changed).

Also observed: after a tablet-side delete plus "empty trash", the content blobs of all three removed documents (`.rm`, `.pdf`, `.epub`) were still downloadable from `/sync/v3/files/<hash>` minutes later, given their index hashes. Orphaned blobs are not purged immediately, which gives the mirror a second chance to fetch content it missed, but nothing says for how long. The kill switch worked as designed: it halted on the two unexpected removals until they were traced to the operator's trash.

Also observed: the device stores a non-palette highlighter colour in `extraMetadata.LastHighlighterv2ColorCode` as a decimal ARGB (`4294951820` = `#FFFFC38C`, the orange highlighter), consistent with the per-stroke `color_rgba` seen in S3.

## Minimal `.content` for a notebook (step 6)

```json
{"fileType": "notebook", "formatVersion": 2, "orientation": "portrait", "pageCount": N,
 "cPages": {"pages": [{"id": "<page uuid>", "idx": {"timestamp": "1:2", "value": "ba"}}, ...],
            "original": {"timestamp": "0:0", "value": -1},
            "lastOpened": {"timestamp": "1:1", "value": "<first page uuid>"}}}
```

`.metadata` needs `visibleName`, `type`, `parent`, `deleted`, `lastModified`, `createdTime`, `pinned`, `synced`, `metadatamodified`, `modified`, `version`, `lastOpened`, `lastOpenedPage`. For `push_markdown`/`push_file` (PDF) the existing `upload_document` layout (`.pagedata` of `Blank` lines plus the PDF blob) is used; the PDF `.content` seen on the device additionally carries `cPages.pages[].redir` and `dummyDocument`.

## Decisions

- G5 stands: restore re-creates documents under their **original UUIDs** by uploading the archived blobs unchanged; the device accepts them and rebuilds its own `.content`/`.metadata` extras.
- Worker writes stay as implemented in the fork: content-addressed blob upload, document index, root upsert by UUID, generation-gated commit with retry that re-applies the mutation (verified against a real 412).
- `push_*` builds `.content` from the minimal set above; nothing more is required for the Move.
- Clean-up: the scratch folder still holds the S1 copy, the minimal copy and two one-page race notebooks. `race B` stays unopened as S4's 50-day probe; the rest can be deleted by the operator.
